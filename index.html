Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about True 5G standalone architecture, plans, and installation.

What makes Jio True 5G "Standalone" (SA) different from other 5G networks?

▼
Unlike Non-Standalone (NSA) 5G networks that route traffic through an older 4G core, Jio's True 5G network is built from the ground up as a pure 5G Standalone (SA) core. This unlocks genuine sub-3ms latency, zero packet degradation, network slicing for high-bandwidth gaming, and superior spectrum efficiency with the 700 MHz (n28) low-band for deep indoor penetration.

Do I need a new 5G SIM card to access Jio True 5G?

▼
No! Any existing Jio 4G SIM or eSIM automatically connects to the True 5G network as long as you have a 5G compatible smartphone with the latest manufacturer software update enabled. Simply turn on 5G Auto in your mobile network settings.

How does JioAirFiber work if optical fiber is not available at my home?

▼
JioAirFiber overcomes physical cabling hurdles by using fixed wireless access (FWA) over dedicated 5G millimetre waves. A compact outdoor receiver communicates directly with the nearest 5G tower and feeds a high-speed Wi-Fi 6 router inside your home, delivering up to 1 Gbps internet speeds without laying a single trench or wire.

Which OTT subscriptions are included in Jio prepaid and fiber recharges?

▼
Depending on your selected plan, Jio bundles subscriptions for Netflix (Basic/Standard), Amazon Prime Video, Disney+ Hotstar, JioCinema Premium, SonyLIV, ZEE5, SunNXT, Hoichoi, Discovery+, Lionsgate Play, and 10+ additional regional streaming platforms at no additional cost.
